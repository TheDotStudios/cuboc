Engineering for Reality - 18 Hours of Battery.
For Displays That Cannot Go Dark.

The Challenge

A client operated in environments where power outages were frequent and branded displays going dark mid-shift was unacceptable. Standard battery tabletops offered 4–6 hours of backup — nowhere near enough for the operational demands of a full working shift.

What We Did

We redesigned the power architecture to deliver 18 hours of backup — enough to survive a full shift plus a full outage without plugging in. We also developed a no-battery variant for budget-sensitive customers who wanted the form factor without the battery system.

The Result

The client got displays that stayed lit when they needed them most. The catalogue got two new variants — premium battery-backed, and no-battery — built to serve completely different customer segments.

What These Stories Have in Common

None of these projects were in our catalogue. Most of them weren't in anyone's catalogue. Every one of them started with a customer asking for something we hadn't built before. That's the work.
