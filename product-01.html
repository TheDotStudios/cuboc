About Cuboc Display

Fabric Light Boxes - FABx Range

Modular fabric systems that assemble in minutes, pack into a car boot, and look like a million rupees. Three configurations for three kinds of environments.

Get a Quote Walk Through One in Our Virtual Showroom

Traditional exhibition booths are heavy. They need specialists to assemble. They barely survive one show before they're damaged. They take up an entire truck to transport.Fabric light boxes changed all that. No tools. No specialists. Pack flat. Set up in minutes. Illuminated edge to edge. Reusable for years. We brought this category to India. Today we manufacture the full range under the FABx brand — three systems, one integrated product line.
