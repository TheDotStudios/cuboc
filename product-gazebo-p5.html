Gazebo Tents · Three Sizes

Outdoor Branding. Up in Minutes. Down in Minutes.

Steel-frame gazebo tents engineered for outdoor activations, exhibitions, pop-ups, and event spaces. Three sizes for three scales of event. Customisable prints, all-weather-ready, and built to be reused — show after show after show.

Get a Quote Compare All Three Sizes

Outdoor events have a specific problem: the venue is usually open sky, the branding needs to be visible from a distance, and the setup window is always shorter than the client promised. Gazebo tents solve all three. A sturdy steel frame that pops up in minutes. A custom-printed canopy that becomes the brand. Weather protection that holds up whether it's sun, rain, or wind. And they fold back down just as fast when the event ends. We manufacture three sizes — each built to the same quality standard, each fully customisable, each ready for repeated use.

WHY CHOOSE CUBOC GAZEBOS

What You're Actually Buying

One manufacturing standard, three sizes
The same quality process runs across all three variants. The 3×3 and the 6×3 are built to the same standard — just sized for different events.
Built for repeat deployment, not one-time use
Most gazebos in the market are sold as consumables. Ours are built to survive multiple events, multiple setups, multiple cities. The economics work in your favour from event two onwards.
Customisation is the default, not an add-on
Canopy prints, colours, roof styles — all customisable from the first order. You're not buying a stock product with a sticker. You're buying your brand, engineered into a gazebo.
Packaging that travels
Padded carry bags. Designed to survive transit between venues. Easy to load, easy to unload, easy to store between events.
