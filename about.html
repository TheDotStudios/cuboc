Our Journey

It started with a problem.

Cuboc began as an importer of roll-up standees. For a few years, it worked. Then the cracks showed — long lead times, stock-outs at the worst moments, shaky quality, prices that moved without notice, no replacements when things broke, zero customisation.

So we changed the model.

We stopped importing. We started manufacturing — in our own factory, under our own roof, with our own quality standards. For the first time, we had control over pricing, availability, quality, and customisation. What came in on the order form was what went out on the truck.

Then customers started asking.

Standard products sell. But the questions kept coming: "What else do you have?" "Can you make this work differently?" "Have you seen what's happening in China?"

We brought it home. We built it better.

We went to a Guangzhou exhibition. We saw fabric light boxes — modular, portable, high-impact — filling stall after stall. The Indian market had nothing like it. That's how the FABx range was born.

Where we are today.

A 14,000 sq. ft. factory. 50+ workers. Daily output of 2,000 standees and 1,000 tabletop displays. Customers in 38 cities across 90% of Indian states. Four industry-first or India-only claims to our name — and a genuine commitment to being the display partner our clients can count on.

Something new to sell. Less risk to carry. More confidence to grow.

Innovative, differentiated products that help you stand apart in your market. In-house manufacturing, stable pricing, dependable availability, and replacement support — so you never carry the uncertainty of selling something new.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Learn more
01.

A women-led manufacturing line

Our entire roll-up standee production line is run by women. No male workers involved in that line. It's not a pilot, it's not a gesture — it's the permanent production standard for that product. We did this because inclusive employment isn't a CSR footnote. It's how the factory actually operates.

02.

A factory with a code of conduct

Foul language, abusive behaviour, and violence are not tolerated inside our walls. Full stop. Dignity, respect, and safety aren't posters on the wall. They're how we expect every supervisor and worker to treat every other supervisor and worker — every day.

03.

Fixing what needed fixing

The road outside our factory used to flood every monsoon. Workers arrived wet. The smell was worse. Hygiene suffered. So we led the initiative to get the road reconstructed — along with other factories in the lane. Now it's clean, safe, and accessible. Our workers don't think about it anymore. Which is the point.

04.

What we actually stand for

Customer First — the customer's problem is the work, not a distraction from it. Innovation With Purpose — we build what solves real problems, not what looks good in a catalogue. Quality and Reliability — the same standard, every order, for every customer. Long-Term Partnerships — we grow with our clients, not past them.
