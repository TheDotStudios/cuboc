203 Versions to Get One Right.

The Challenge

A client needed a standee that was heavier, more stable, and more durable than anything in the market. Every off-the-shelf option failed their requirement.

What We Did

We didn't guess. We iterated. Our team developed and tested 203 different variations of the standee — adjusting weight, base structure, material thickness, and stability until we had a version that met the client's specific use case. Each variant was presented back for evaluation.

The Result

A custom heavy-duty standee built for long-term, high-stability deployment. The client got a
product that actually solved their problem — not a standard product with a new sticker.
