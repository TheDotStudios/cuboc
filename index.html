About

We Stopped Importing. We Started Building.

For years, the display industry ran on imports. Long lead times. Shaky quality. No customisation. Prices set by someone else. We chose differently. In-house manufacturing. Modular thinking. Products engineered for how Indian exhibitions and retail actually work — not how the brochure says they should.

Today, Cuboc is a manufacturing-led display company with one of India's widest modular ranges, supplying 300+ customers across 90% of Indian states.
