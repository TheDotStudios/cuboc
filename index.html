All Properties

Fabric Light Boxes

Fabric Light Boxes

Booths that assemble in minutes, pack into a car boot. Modular fabric systems for exhibitions, retail environments, and brand activations. Tool-free setup. Reusable across campaigns. FABxGO, FABxGLOW, FABxPRO — three systems, one integrated range.

Standees

Standees

The everyday display, engineered properly. Five variants from regular promotional units to premium chrome and modular cube systems. Smooth roll mechanisms. Stable structure. Bulk-order ready, consistent across every batch.

Ultra Thin Glass Frames

Ultra Thin Glass Frames

When the frame should disappear into the wall. Sleek, premium frames for showrooms, offices, and retail interiors. Wall-mounted, backlit, or custom-sized. Tabletop and ceiling accessories available.

Battery Table Tops

Battery Table Tops

Plug-free branding for counters and kiosks. Compact, portable, battery-operated. Some variants with 18-hour backup. Built for retail counters, reception desks, and small spaces where running a wire isn't an option.

Gazebo Tents

Gazebo Tents

Outdoor branding, up in minutes, down in minutes. Steel-frame gazebo tents for outdoor events, activations, and pop-ups. Three sizes — 3.0×3.0 m, 4.5×3.0 m, 6.0×3.0 m. Customisable prints, all-weather protection, quick setup and teardown.
