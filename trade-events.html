Event 1

First Major Showcase.
18 Square Metres.

Media Expo Delhi · 2023

Our first serious industry presence. An 18 sq. m stall that put Cuboc in front of a national audience for the first time. The stall was small. The learning wasn't.

  • First real exposure to buyers outside our existing network
  • Direct feedback on product range from fabricators, agencies, and dealers who'd never seen our work before
  • The first market signals that told us what to build next — including the demand for modular fabric systems that later became FABx

This show validated that we were on the right track. It also showed us how far we had to go.

Mar 07
Event 2

Same Show.
3.5× The Floor Space.

Media Expo Delhi · 2025

Two years later, we returned with a 64 sq. m stall — more than three times our 2023 footprint. The size wasn't the statement. The product range was.

  • Full FABx series on display — the modular fabric system that was still an idea in 2023
  • Live demonstrations of tool-free setup on exhibition-grade booths
  • Complete standee range across five variants, including the Cube Standee
  • First public showing of customisation capability at scale

Every customer conversation confirmed the same thing: the market wanted what we'd built. We just needed to make sure they knew it existed.
