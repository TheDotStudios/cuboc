When the Frame Should Disappear Into the Wall.

Ultra Thin Glass Frames

Sleek, premium glass frames for retail interiors, showrooms, and office environments. Minimal frame. Maximum display. Available in wall-mounted, backlit, and custom configurations.

Get a Quote See Mounting Options

Most display frames are the opposite of minimal. Thick edges. Visible hardware. Bulky profile. The frame dominates the wall instead of the message it's supposed to hold. Our Ultra Thin Glass Frames do the opposite. An ultra-thin profile. Clean lines. The frame disappears and the graphic does the talking. Ideal for environments where aesthetic matters as much as information.

WHY CHOOSE ULTRA THIN GLASS FRAMES

Where They Actually Shine.

Built for the Spaces That Notice the Details.

In luxury retail

Where the store's visual environment is as important as the product. The thin frame doesn't fight with the interior designer's vocabulary.

In showrooms and corporate offices

Where a generic display poster looks wrong. Our frames feel considered, intentional, designed.

In premium branding environments

Where customers are paying for the experience as much as the product. Display shouldn't cheapen the space.
